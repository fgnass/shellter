Privacy

The app

Shellter runs only on your Mac. It is a terminal: what you type goes to the shells and programs in its tabs, as in any other terminal.

  • No command, output or other content is sent to me or to a server of mine.
  • The app has no analytics, no crash reports and no update checks.
  • To find the favicon of a dev server and to see which ports serve a web page, Shellter sends requests to localhost on your Mac. These requests do not leave your Mac.
  • To show the status of a tab, its icon and its port, Shellter looks at the processes of its tabs and at the files in their folders. To show the windows of other apps that belong to a project, it uses the Accessibility permission, if you give it. It reads only which file a window shows.
  • To continue the session of a coding agent, Shellter reads the session ID from the agent’s own files on your Mac (for example in ~/.claude or ~/.codex).
  • The programs that you run in Shellter can connect to the internet, as in any terminal. What they do is outside of Shellter.

What stays on your Mac

Shellter keeps its data in ~/Library/Application Support/Shellter.

  • state.json keeps your tabs: their folders, titles, order and the session IDs of coding agents.
  • The zmx sessions of the tabs keep the screen and the scrollback of each tab in memory, so that they come back after Shellter restarts. A restart of the Mac clears them. When you close a tab, its session ends.
  • Your settings (font, theme, sidebar, project colors) are kept in the app’s preferences and in appearance.config.
